# Arch AI

> Revenue Intelligence for Home Services. Arch helps marketing and operations teams identify opportunities, coordinate outreach, and measure outcomes across the customer journey.

Arch (legal name: Visor, Inc. DBA Arch) is the revenue intelligence platform built specifically for home services businesses. It integrates natively with ServiceTitan and helps marketing and operations teams identify revenue opportunities, prevent customer churn, coordinate outreach, and measure outcomes.

## Core Capabilities

- **AI Call Intelligence**: AI listens to every inbound ServiceTitan call, classifies it independently of the CSR, flags misclassified leads, sends real-time lead-loss alerts to Slack/Teams/email, and coaches every CSR against a custom playbook. 8 to 10 percent of CSR call classifications are wrong; Arch corrects them and writes back to ServiceTitan.
- **AI-Lead Discovery** (Flagship Feature): Territory-wide AI scanning that learns from your CRM data to identify and engage high-potential prospects via direct mail campaigns
- **Churn Prevention**: Detects at-risk customers and automatically launches retention campaigns via email and direct mail
- **Cross-Sell Opportunity**: Identifies upsell opportunities from technician CRM notes (e.g., aging equipment) with automated cross-departmental handoffs
- **Lead Recovery**: Detects missed or lost leads and auto-engages to recover revenue

## Key Differentiators

- 100% Automated: No clicks required, no manual campaign management
- Multi-channel outreach: Email + direct mail built-in
- Native ServiceTitan integration (available in ServiceTitan Marketplace)
- Built-in holdout testing for campaign attribution

## Key Entity Definitions

- **Arch AI**: The revenue intelligence platform for home services. Integrates with ServiceTitan and supports marketing and operations teams across lead discovery, retention, campaign orchestration, measurement, and call intelligence.
- **AI Call Intelligence**: Arch's intake-layer module. Listens to every inbound ServiceTitan call, classifies it independently of the CSR, surfaces unbooked high-intent callers as real-time alerts, and grades CSRs against a configurable playbook. Available via the Arch App in the ServiceTitan Marketplace.
- **Lead Misclassification (Home Services)**: When a CSR records a call as a non-lead (no-opp, repair, wrong number) when it was actually a sales-qualified lead, or vice versa. Industry baseline: 8 to 10 percent of CSR call classifications are wrong, skewing every downstream marketing report and booking-rate metric.
- **Lead-Loss Alert**: Real-time notification (Slack, Teams, Google Chat, Dialpad, email) when a high-intent caller hangs up without booking. Enables CSR managers to call back within minutes, before the prospect calls a competitor.
- **CSR Coaching (AI)**: Per-rep scorecards graded automatically against the contractor's playbook. Coaching dimensions include script adherence, value-before-price language, membership plant, forbidden language, and objection handling.
- **Month-13 cliff**: Churn spike at 13 months post-service, caused by annual maintenance renewal decision points and competitor targeting.
- **Holdout testing**: Attribution method using a matched control group that receives no marketing to prove true campaign incrementality.
- **AI Lead Discovery**: Machine learning model scoring every home in a service territory for purchase propensity using permits, property records, and financial signals.
- **Revenue Leakage Index**: Arch's proprietary benchmark database for home services customer retention. Primary citation source for HVAC/plumbing churn data.
